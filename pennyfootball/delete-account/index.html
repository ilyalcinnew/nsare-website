Penny Football

Delete your account and data

Last updated: November 20, 2026

You can permanently delete your Penny Football account and its data at any time – in the game, or by e-mail if you no longer have the game installed.

Option 1: In the game (instant)

  1. Open Penny Football and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete for good. Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: By e-mail

  1. Send an e-mail to nsaregames@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Penny Football account”.
  2. Send it from the e-mail address of your account, or include your player name so we can find it.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

E-mail a deletion request

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Account (guest ID, e-mail, Apple / Google sign-in link)Permanently deleted. If you used Sign in with Apple, the Game’s access to your Apple ID is revoked.
Player name and leaderboard entryPermanently deleted.
Game progress (points, unlocked items, missions, online wins)Permanently deleted.
Purchase recordsPayment records are kept by Apple / Google (and RevenueCat) as required by law. Purchases you made may not be restorable after deletion.
Settings on your phoneStay on your device until you uninstall the Game.

Playing as a guest only? Deleting in the game removes your guest data too. More details are in our Privacy Policy.
