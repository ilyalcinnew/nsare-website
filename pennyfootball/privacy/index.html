2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. There is no chat in the Game. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Service providers

We use these companies to run the Game. They process data only to provide their service:

  • Supabase – database and sign-in (servers in the EU, Frankfurt). Privacy policy
  • Resend – sends the sign-in code e-mail. Privacy policy
  • Apple and Google – Sign in with Apple / Google, app stores and payments.
  • Google AdMob – ads. How Google uses data in ads
  • RevenueCat – manages in-app purchases. Privacy policy

4. Ads and consent

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob (for example, an optional rewarded ad for extra points, and an ad between matches). The Game requests non-personalised ads only and does not ask for permission to track you across other apps. Ads are limited to a family-friendly content rating.

Google may still use your device’s advertising ID and ad interaction data for things like limiting how often you see an ad, measuring ads and preventing fraud. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google may appear before ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Buying “Remove Ads” turns off the ads between matches.

5. In-app purchases

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or payment details. RevenueCat receives your Game account ID and the purchase record so your purchases can be delivered and restored on your devices.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account and progress for as long as your account exists.
  • Delete in the Game: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, player name, progress and related data from our servers.
  • If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the Game’s access to your Apple ID.
  • You can also ask us by e-mail – see how to delete your account.
  • Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat may be retained by them as required for accounting and legal reasons.

7. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our database is restricted so that each player can only change their own data.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or Canada’s PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict processing. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top always shows the latest version. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the Game.
