Nail Football

Delete your account and data

Last updated: November 20, 2026

You can permanently delete your Nail Football account and all data linked to it at any time – in the game, or by e-mail if you no longer have the game installed.

Option 1: In the game (instant)

  1. Open Nail Football and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account, profile and match history are deleted right away.

Option 2: By e-mail

  1. Send an e-mail to nsaregames@gmail.com from the e-mail address you registered with.
  2. Use the subject “Delete my Nail Football account”.
  3. We confirm it is your account, delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.
