1. Data we collect

Creating an account collects only these fields:

DataWhy
E-mail addressSigns you in and lets you recover your account. Never shown to other players.
PasswordStored by our sign-in provider (Supabase Auth) in hashed form. We never see it in plain text.
UsernameYour public name on the leaderboard and in matches.
Country (optional)Shown as a flag next to your leaderboard entry. You can leave it blank.
Chosen coloursColour your disc and goal in matches, and your leaderboard row.
Language preferenceWhich of the Game’s 7 languages you see.
Scores and win preferenceYour total score and goals needed to win, for the leaderboard.

In addition:

  • Online match data (room code, moves and match state) is used to run a match between two players. Rooms are temporary and are cleaned up when they end or expire.
  • Advertising data (your device’s advertising ID and ad interaction data) is used by Google AdMob to show ads – see section 5.
  • Server logs (IP address and timestamps) are kept briefly by our hosting provider for security and abuse prevention.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Guest play

You can play without registering by choosing Continue as Guest. A guest has no e-mail or password: our sign-in provider issues an anonymous session ID, and a random username (for example “SwiftStriker4821”) is created so you can still appear on the leaderboard.

3. How we use it

  • Sign-in – your e-mail and password sign you back in on any device.
  • Gameplay – your colours and language set up the match you see; match data runs online games.
  • Leaderboard – your username, country, colours and score build the global rankings.
  • Ads – to show ads that keep the Game free (section 5).

4. The leaderboard is public

Your username, country, colours and total score can be seen by anyone who opens the leaderboard. Your e-mail address is never shown to other players.

If you prefer not to appear with an identifying name, play as a guest or choose a username that does not identify you.

5. Ads and consent

The Game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner and an ad after some single-player matches. Ads are limited to a family-friendly content rating (G).

  • Google may use your device’s advertising ID and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. This can include personalised ads where you allow it.
  • In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown.
  • On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used for tracking if you allow it when asked (App Tracking Transparency). If you are not asked or say no, it is not used to personalise ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone’s settings.

Learn more: How Google uses data in ads.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase – sign-in and database. Privacy policy
  • Google AdMob – ads. Privacy policy
  • Google Play and Apple App Store – app delivery and updates.

7. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your account and profile for as long as your account exists.
  • Delete in the Game: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, profile and match history.
  • Guest accounts are removed automatically.
  • You can also ask us by e-mail – see how to delete your account. E-mail requests are processed within 30 days.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Change your username, country, colours, language and win preference at any time in Settings.
  • Play without an account using Guest mode.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or Canada’s PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to processing. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top always shows the latest version. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the Game.
