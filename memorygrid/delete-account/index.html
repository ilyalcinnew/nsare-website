Memory Grid

Delete your account and data

Last updated: November 20, 2026

You can ask us to permanently delete your Memory Grid account and all data linked to it at any time, whether or not you still have the game installed.

Delete it in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Memory Grid and tap Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  3. Your account and all its data are deleted immediately. If the game asks you to log in again first, log in and repeat step 2.

Or request deletion by e-mail

  1. Send an e-mail to nsaregames@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Memory Grid account”.
  2. Send it from the e-mail address you registered with. If you played as a guest, include your username.
  3. We confirm it is your account, delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.
