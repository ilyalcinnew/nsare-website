Sound settings and your current game are saved only on your device. We do not collect your location or contacts, and the Game does not use your microphone or camera. We do not sell your personal data. There are no in-app purchases.

2. Profile picture

If you choose a photo as your avatar, the Game asks for access to your photo library and lets you pick one picture. The photo itself is not uploaded to our servers; only a reference to the file on your phone is saved with your profile. You can switch to an emoji avatar at any time.

3. The leaderboard is public

Your username, avatar and total score can be seen by other players on the leaderboard. Your e-mail address is not shown to other players.

4. Ads

The Game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner and an ad between some games. Ads are limited to a family-friendly content rating (G). Google may use your device’s advertising ID and ad interaction data to show and measure ads (which can include personalised ads), limit how often you see them and prevent fraud.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone’s settings. Learn more: How Google uses data in ads.

5. Service providers

  • Google Firebase – sign-in (Firebase Authentication) and database (Realtime Database). Privacy and security in Firebase
  • Google AdMob – ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play – app delivery and updates.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your profile and scores for as long as your account exists. Online match rooms are deleted when the match ends.
  • To delete your account and all its data, send us a request by e-mail – see how to delete your account. We process requests within 30 days.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or Canada’s PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to processing. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
